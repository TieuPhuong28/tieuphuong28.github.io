Yoko Onsen Quang Hanh is a Japanese-inspired hot spring resort in Quang Ninh, Vietnam. The brand identity combines Japanese wellness traditions with a refined, minimalist aesthetic, creating a sense of tranquility, balance and premium relaxation.

## Concept

Pronounced "Yōko", the name conveys a sense of sunshine and warmth. Combining "Yoko" with "Onsen" evokes the image of a sunlit hot spring, symbolising the serenity, vitality and restorative essence of the onsen experience.

The name reflects the spirit of sunshine and warmth, connecting the brand to Sun Group – Sun World while embodying the holistic relaxation and rejuvenation that the onsen experience brings to both body and mind.
