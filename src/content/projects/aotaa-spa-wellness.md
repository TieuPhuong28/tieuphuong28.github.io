AoTaa Spa & Wellness is a wellness brand inspired by the simplicity of Vietnamese traditions and the tranquil beauty of nature. The visual identity draws on the familiar image of a traditional Vietnamese pond, evoking nostalgia, warmth and a connection to one's roots. Through organic forms, natural tones and refined typography, the brand speaks a harmonious visual language of relaxation, balance and holistic well-being.

## The process

The concept comes from the image of the village pond ("ao ta") in the folk verse *"Ta về ta tắm ao ta, dù trong dù đục ao nhà vẫn hơn"* — there's no place like home, no matter how clear or murky its waters. It evokes familiarity, warmth and comfort, inviting people to visit and experience its charm.

For generations, the pond has symbolised a connection to one's roots: a cherished place filled with memories, heartfelt emotions and the quiet beauty of a bygone era. Though rooted in the past, it remains deeply familiar, bringing a comforting sense of nostalgia whenever we look back.
