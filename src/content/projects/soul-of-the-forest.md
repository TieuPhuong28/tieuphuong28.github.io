Soul of the Forest is a series of music events held amidst the pine forests of Flamingo Dai Lai Resort. Inspired by the harmony of music and nature, the project creates an immersive atmosphere where art, sound and the natural landscape come together to deliver unique and memorable experiences.

## The challenge

Launched in 2021, Soul of the Forest has grown over nearly five years into one of Northern Vietnam's most prominent music shows. To keep the experience fresh and the brand relevant, the show's visual identity was refreshed to reflect contemporary creative trends while preserving its distinctive spirit and character.
